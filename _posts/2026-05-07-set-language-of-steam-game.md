内容参考这篇文章: [Reddit - change language without steam](https://www.reddit.com/r/Enshrouded/comments/1aeunlh/change_language_without_steam/)

* 打开游戏目录
* 进入 `steam_settings` 文件夹
* 打开 `force_language.txt` 文件, 如果没有这个文件就创建一个
* 把文件内容修改为对应的语言代码, 比如简体中文为 `schinese`, 具体的代码列表为 [Languages Supported on Steam (Steamworks Documentation)](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/localization/languages)
